FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between Matter and Thread?

Matter is the application language that lets devices speak across Apple, Alexa, and Google, while Thread is the low-power wireless mesh radio protocol that transmits those messages without a hub.

Are your ratings influenced by manufacturers?

Never. We purchase retail equipment directly or run anonymous hardware batches. Editorial scores are governed solely by our published 10-point testing criteria.
