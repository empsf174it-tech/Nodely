Engineering Notes

Understanding Connectivity Protocols

Essential background on how wireless standards govern speed, reliability, and local responsiveness.

Matter Standard

Developed by the Connectivity Standards Alliance (CSA), Matter is an application protocol running over Wi-Fi, Ethernet, and Thread. It eliminates cloud hops for local control across Apple, Alexa, Google, and Home Assistant.

Thread Mesh Radio

A low-power, self-healing IPv6 wireless mesh operating on 2.4GHz 802.15.4 radio. Unlike Wi-Fi, adding more mains-powered Thread devices strengthens the entire network mesh rather than congesting your router.

Multi-Admin Commissioning

Matter's signature capability: once paired to your Apple Home, you can issue an ephemeral cryptographic setup token to invite Amazon Alexa or Google Home to share local control simultaneously.
